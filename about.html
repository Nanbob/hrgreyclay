Hello, I'm Hannah.

HRGrey Clay is the ceramics project of me, Hannah Grey; a Liverpool based, Derby born potter.

Shapes, colours and textures are inspired by days out in the Peak District and close up snapshots of nature – whether that’s mossy barks or marine lichen at beaches. My practice is mainly wheel based, creating functional and decorative pieces that I hope are loved and used as part of daily life.

I can’t escape my love of cats; and often turn recycled clay into experimental/weird cat-based mini figures 🐈‍⬛ Every piece is stamped with my cat based makers mark. Each piece is made by hand, so no two are quite the same. Feel free to email me or get in touch on Instagram if there's anything you'd like to order.

Thanks for stopping by and taking a look at my work.
